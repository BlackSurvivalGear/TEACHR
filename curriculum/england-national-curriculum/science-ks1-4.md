# England National Curriculum — Science

TEACHR's Science curriculum intelligence is derived from the Department for Education's official **National curriculum in England: science programmes of study**.

Authoritative source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study

The source is statutory guidance and applies to England. It provides year-by-year programmes for Key Stages 1 and 2 and key-stage programmes for Key Stages 3 and 4. TEACHR preserves that structure rather than fabricating statutory year-by-year sequences for KS3 or KS4.

The JSON dataset contains concise TEACHR-controlled paraphrases of the statutory programme. It does not reproduce the source verbatim, and non-statutory notes, examples and suggestions are not represented as statutory objectives.
