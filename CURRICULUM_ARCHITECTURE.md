# TEACHR Curriculum Architecture

The workspace curriculum selector distinguishes the four UK education systems rather than treating them as one curriculum.

- England — National Curriculum
- Scotland — Curriculum for Excellence
- Wales — Curriculum for Wales
- Northern Ireland — Northern Ireland Curriculum

The selector changes the available subject/area, stage and topic options together.

Sources: GOV.UK National Curriculum; Education Scotland Curriculum for Excellence; Hwb Curriculum for Wales; Northern Ireland Department of Education statutory curriculum.
