# TEACHR VLE — Stage 1

## Scope
- Signed-in workspace entry for TEACHR VLE.
- Dedicated `vle.html` shell.
- England National Curriculum active.
- Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland visible as disabled Coming soon options.
- Early Years / Reception through Key Stage 4, Years Reception–11.
- Subject and curriculum-area filters.
- Minimal subject-module placeholders.
- VLE shell is independent of generation usage and paywall code.

## Manual verification
1. Sign in to TEACHR and confirm the TEACHR VLE card appears in the signed-in workspace.
2. Open the card and confirm `vle.html` loads.
3. Confirm signed-out or unverified users see the sign-in-required state.
4. Switch EYFS/KS1/KS2/KS3/KS4 and confirm Year options map to Reception/1–2/3–6/7–9/10–11.
5. Switch all six subjects and confirm curriculum-area options update.
6. Confirm Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland are disabled and labelled Coming soon.
7. Confirm all six subject cards are minimal Coming soon placeholders.
8. Return to Workspace and smoke-test all six existing generator cards plus Resource Library.
9. Confirm entering VLE does not change free-generation usage.
10. Check desktop and mobile layouts.
